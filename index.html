How We Rate Accommodations

Every property is scored on the same checklist. A high score means the features below are present, measured, and confirmed by real guests.

  1. Step-free entrance and accessible parking
  2. Elevator access and wide hallways
  3. Roll-in showers and grab bars
  4. Visual and hearing alerts, such as flashing fire alarms
  5. Trained staff and a clear accessibility contact

Featured Accessible Stays

These sample properties show how we present our ratings. Each one is reviewed by guests who use wheelchairs, mobility aids, or other accessibility support.

Strawberry Inn

Accessibility rating: 4.8 out of 5

The Strawberry Inn is a boutique lodging experience nestled in the forested town of Strawberry, Arizona. Among their variety of cabins and cottages is their accessible Cottage 11, a fully wheelchair-accessible cabin that truly reflects thoughtful and functional design.

They have: step-free entry, roll-in showers, thoughtful bathroom design, and staff trained in guest assistance.

Book directly with the Strawberry Inn here

Sedona Cabins

Accessibility rating: 3.9 out of 5

This wheelchair accessible cabin has a front porch views of the grape vines, a vegetable garden and the red rocks down the canyon in Sedona. There are good room layouts, but the vegetable garden doesn't have a paved path and parking is far from the lobby.

Book with Sedona Cabins here

Arizona Biltmore

Accessibility rating: 1.6 out of 5

The room is on the first floor but there are stairs at every entrance and no elevator. The amenities that they advertise aren't fully accessible if you have a wheelchair or mobility impairment.
